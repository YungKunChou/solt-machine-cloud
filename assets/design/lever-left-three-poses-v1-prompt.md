## 初稿提示

Create ONE photorealistic three-panel mechanical pose comparison sheet, landscape 1536x1024 or wider. Input image is the reference for EXACT appearance/materials of the LEFT red-ball slot machine lever and adjacent left cabinet edge. This is a focused study of the same left lever in three stages of being pulled TOWARD THE VIEWER, not sideways.
COMPOSITION: three equally wide panels, pure warm-white background, generous margins, no text, no arrows, no borders. Same fixed camera, scale, crop and lighting in every panel. Each panel shows the complete LEFT lever assembly plus a narrow slice of the machine's LEFT vertical brass/silver cabinet edge to its RIGHT, enough cabinet edge for context; omit the rest of the machine. All three fixed axle centers must align at exactly the same screen height about 72% down the sheet; fixed cabinet edges and fixed brass bearing mount identical in all panels. Every red ball fully visible. Use realistic aged brass, polished silver cylindrical axle sleeve and glossy deep red ball matching the reference closely. Nothing schematic or stylized.
MECHANICS: The axle extends horizontally leftward from the cabinet. The cylinder axis is left-right, NOT pointing at the camera. A metal rod is rigidly attached radially to a rotating silver collar on that axle. There is a stationary brass bearing flange against the cabinet. When pulled, the silver collar and attached socket rotate together about the axle toward camera and downward. Socket starts on TOP of cylinder and travels over its FRONT curved face. Bearing flange and axle center never move, sag or tilt. Fixed outer rim overlaps appropriate edges of rotating collar for depth.
LEFT PANEL: upright at 0 degrees, matches reference left lever: tall slim slightly leaned brass rod, spherical red ball above, socket emerging at the TOP of cylinder.
MIDDLE PANEL: pulled 35 degrees forward toward the viewer. Ball moves down and slightly closer (about 3% larger), stays perfectly round. Rod remains rigid, appears foreshortened by perspective, not telescoped. CRUCIAL socket now visibly rotates toward viewer onto the UPPER FRONT quadrant of silver cylinder, lower than its original top attachment. Show changed socket angle, visible rounded collar face and contact shadow; not merely shorter rod glued to the unchanged top.
RIGHT PANEL: fully pulled 65 degrees forward toward viewer, NOT 90 degrees. Red ball down near but still ABOVE axle center, appears about 7% larger than upright. Rod remains clearly visible between red ball and pivot but is considerably foreshortened. CRUCIAL root socket wraps visibly further DOWN to the FRONT face of cylindrical collar, below its position in middle panel, collar rotates with root. Show coherent depth occlusion and slightly darker contact at socket, preserving polished metal realism. No sideways swing.
INVARIANTS: same single lever, same rod physical length and diameter, same ball design, same bearing, same stationary cabinet edge and unchanged stationary lighting in all panels. Only physical articulation and consequent highlights/occlusion vary. Do not rotate entire base as a flat disk; do not leave root socket pinned to top across all poses; do not flatten or squash sphere; no duplicated rod remnants; no other levers; no text/watermark. This is a visual key-pose proposal, not engineering diagram.
